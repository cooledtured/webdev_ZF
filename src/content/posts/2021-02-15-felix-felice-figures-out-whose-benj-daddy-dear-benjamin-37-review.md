📖 ABOUT

Isaac is the owner of a small flowershop in San Diego visited by the famous arms dealer, Felix, late one night. The arms dealer ordered a flower bouquet and Isaac handed him a clumsily arranged one, but for some reason, Felix showed unusual interest towards Isaac.

Felix, a man with a dangerous charm who visits the flower shop almost everyday, and florist Isaac, a man with a lot of secrets who acts indifferent... Will Felix ever discover what Isaac is hiding?
