## Anime Central 2026: From Guardians of the Galaxy to One Piece: Sitting Down With David Sobolov

Welcome back to the Zealed Fujoshi podcast! In this episode, CeeCee sits down with the incredible David Sobolov to discuss his legendary voice acting career. We dive deep into his iconic role as Kaido in One Piece, exploring how he mastered the "Worororo" laugh, the vocal limits he pushed during the epic Gear 5 battle in the Wano arc, and why he views the Emperor of the Sea as a deeply misunderstood character. David also shares funny memories of voicing Drax in the Guardians of the Galaxy animated series and teases a massive, top-secret project coming in 2026!About Our Guest:David Sobolov is a veteran voice actor known for his deep, commanding voice across animation, anime, and video games. He is widely celebrated by the anime community as the English voice of Kaido, the "Strongest Creature," in One Piece. His extensive resume also includes bringing Drax the Destroyer to life in Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy animated series.
