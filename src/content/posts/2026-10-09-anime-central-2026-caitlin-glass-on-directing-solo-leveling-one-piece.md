## Anime Central 2026: Caitlin Glass on Directing Solo Leveling, & One Piece

Welcome back to the Zealed Fujoshi podcast! In this episode, CeeCee interviews the legendary voice actress and ADR Director, Caitlin Glass. We discuss her iconic OG roles like Winry Rockbell (Fullmetal Alchemist) and Haruhi Fujioka (Ouran High School Host Club), and what it's like to voice energetic characters like Mina Ashido in My Hero Academia and grumpy ones like Damian Desmond in Spy x Family. Caitlin also shares incredible behind-the-scenes stories from directing the hit English dub of Solo Leveling, praising Aleks Le's breathtaking and emotional performance as Jinwoo. Plus, we chat about Nefertari Vivi's highly anticipated return in One Piece, her time playing Cammy White in Street Fighter, directing the upcoming Chainsaw Man - The Movie: Reze Arc, and the massive honor of voicing Oscar in The Rose of Versailles!About Our Guest:Caitlin Glass is a prolific voice actress and ADR director with decades of experience in the anime and video game industry. She is widely celebrated for voicing classic characters such as Winry Rockbell in Fullmetal Alchemist and Haruhi Fujioka in Ouran High School Host Club. Her extensive voice acting credits include Nefertari Vivi in One Piece, Mina Ashido in My Hero Academia, Damian Desmond in Spy x Family, Cammy White in the Street Fighter series, and Oscar in The Rose of Versailles.
