## Chat with Captain Kuro: An Interview with Alexander Maniatis | Anime Magic 2024

In this exclusive interview, we delve into the world of One Piece with Alexander Maniatis. From his audition process to his portrayal of the enigmatic Captain Kuro, Maniatis shares his insights and experiences on bringing this iconic character to the live-action screen. We discuss the challenges and rewards of adapting a beloved anime and manga, as well as the impact of the show on fans and the industry.
