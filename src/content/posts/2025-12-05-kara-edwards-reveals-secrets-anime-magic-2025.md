## KARA EDWARDS REVEALS SECRETS | Anime Magic 2025

We sat down with the incredible voice actor Kara Edwards, best known for voicing the child version of Goten and Videl (Great Saiyaman 2) across the entire Dragon Ball Z universe, including Dragon Ball Super, GT, and the eagerly anticipated Dragon Ball Daima. Recorded live at the Chicago-area Anime Magic convention , Kara Edwards shares her journey into voice acting, her time working with Funimation , and the detailed process behind her roles in major video games like Battleborn, Borderlands 3, and Orcs Must Die! 3.
