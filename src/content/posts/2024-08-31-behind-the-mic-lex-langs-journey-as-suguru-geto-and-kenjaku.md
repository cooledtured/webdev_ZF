## Behind the Mic: Lex Lang's Journey as Suguru Geto and Kenjaku

Get ready to be immersed in the world of Jujutsu Kaisen like never before! In this exclusive interview, we sit down with the incredibly talented Lex Lang, the voice behind the complex and captivating characters of Suguru Geto and Kenjaku. Discover the secrets behind his performances, the challenges of voicing such iconic villains, and never-before-heard insights into the minds of these enigmatic characters. Don't miss this opportunity to delve deep into the world of Jujutsu Kaisen with one of its most prominent voice actors.
