## Reinholt X Mikhail = ❤️ | Your Devotion is My Salvation #9 Review

📖 ABOUT

Police officer Reinholt who went to a 5-star hotel for a vacation wanted a one night stand at first sight with a man who introduced himself as “Milo”. In that passing moment when Reinholt thought of how he wanted to have a special relationship with him, he saw Milo with an unusual crowd… Is that guy part of the mafia…? Even when Reinholt teld himself in his mind that he shouldn't, his heart was doing the opposite and it kept reacting to Milo. Kill or be killed… This is the dizzying romance between a police officer and a mafia!
