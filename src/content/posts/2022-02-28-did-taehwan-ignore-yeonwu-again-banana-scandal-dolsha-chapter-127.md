📖 ABOUT

At a university located in a city in the United States, Dojin and Taehwan are friends and housemates. When Taehwan is away to be an exchange student, Taehwan's younger brother, Taehee enters the house to be Dojin's new housemate. Unlike his height and pretty appearance, Taehee is a hikikomori type otaku, but somehow he seems like he's only being nice to Dojin. On the night of celebrating Taehee's arrival, Dojin wakes up with an unidentified sound, and soon afterwards realizes that the source of this sound is Taehee's groan of masturbating...?!
