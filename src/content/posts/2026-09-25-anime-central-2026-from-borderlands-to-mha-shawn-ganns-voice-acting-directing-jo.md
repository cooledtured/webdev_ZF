## Anime Central 2026: From Borderlands to MHA: Shawn Gann's Voice Acting & Directing Journey

Step into the booth with Shawn Gann, a powerhouse Filipino-American voice actor, ADR director, and theater maker! Whether he's voicing Hekiji Tengai in My Hero Academia, lending his vocal talents to legendary hits like One Piece, Dragon Ball Super, and Attack on Titan, or bringing video game characters to life in Borderlands 3 and Genshin Impact, Shawn's versatility is unmatched. Beyond the mic, he is an accomplished Crunchyroll ADR Director, steering the English dubs for major anime like Kaiju No. 8, The Apothecary Diaries, Horimiya, and Buddy Daddies. With a rich background in Dallas-Fort Worth theater and over 150 voiceover credits, Shawn is a master storyteller on screen, on stage, and behind the scenes.About Our Guest:Shawn Gann is a Filipino-American voice actor, ADR director, and theatre maker based in Dallas, Texas, with over 150 voiceover credits to his name. He is widely known for his voice work in hit anime series like My Hero Academia (Hekiji Tengai), One Piece, Dragon Ball Super, and Attack on Titan, as well as video games such as Borderlands 3, Tiny Tina's Wonderlands, and Genshin Impact. As a highly sought-after ADR Director for Crunchyroll, Shawn has helmed the English dubs for massive titles including Kaiju No. 8, The Apothecary Diaries, Horimiya, Buddy Daddies, and How a Realist Hero Rebuilt the Kingdom.
