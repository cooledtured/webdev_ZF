## Anime Central 2026: Creating Disney's Dragon Striker! Sylvain Dos Santos & Charles Lefebvre

Welcome back to the Zealed Fujoshi podcast! In this episode, CeeCee sits down with Sylvain Dos Santos and Charles Lefebvre, the brilliant creators behind the highly anticipated animated series, Dragon Striker. We discuss the incredible US fan reactions to the premiere, the inspiration behind the magical sport of Gorotama, and why the main character, Key, isn't your typical underdog. Sylvain and Charles also dive into the process of blending European fantasy with 2D Japanese anime styles for Disney, working with world-class composer Kevin Penkin (Made in Abyss), and their massive plans for seasons 2, 3, and 4! The first 11 episodes launch on June 6th, so get ready to enter the game.About Our Guest:Sylvain Dos Santos and Charles Lefebvre are the passionate creators and executive producers of Dragon Striker, an epic sports fantasy series. Bringing together their love for Japanese animation and European storytelling, they have developed a unique 2D anime style that stands out on Disney.
